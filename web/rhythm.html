Sounds

Each key plays the sound the engine builds in code, or a sample from the starter pack. A lit switch plays its sample. Tap a name to hear it; tap its switch to change it and hear the change. through fx is lit while the sound goes through the effects; tap it to keep the sound clean. The beat, these choices and the sliders stay in this browser.

Playing it

Hold sound and tap a key to choose a sound. write turns the keys into the chosen sound's sixteen steps. play starts and stops. Hold bpm: a key sets the volume, slider A the swing, slider B the tempo; tap it alone for 80, 120 and 140. Hold function and press pattern to clear. With a keyboard: 1234 qwer asdf zxcv are the keys, 7 8 9 sound, pattern and bpm, u i o p function, fx, play and write. The sliders are your hand: they set every sound, and a step keeps where they were when it was written. The thin line on a slider is what the chosen sound was last played with. Double-click (or double-tap) a slider to put it back to the middle; with bpm held, to swing 0 and 120 bpm. While it plays, hold fx and a key for an effect: it is heard at once, the display says fx and its number, and the steps it plays over keep it (a small mark above each). Hold fx alone to take the effects off the steps it passes. Sixteen patterns: hold pattern and tap a key to choose one; keep holding and tap more keys to chain them, in order, up to sixteen. While it plays, the change waits for the end of the bar. Hold write and pattern and tap a key to copy the pattern playing there. function and pattern clear the pattern playing, and only it. While pattern is held the keys are the patterns: lit ones hold something, the one playing flickers, the corner square marks the chain. The display says which pattern plays, where the chain is (2/4) and what waits (→ 5). With a mouse or one hand: Shift+click or Shift+key sound, pattern, bpm or function to hold it down; the next key lets it go (so Shift+click sound, click 16), or Shift it again. Shift+fx stays on until you Shift it again: each key then plays that effect for as long as you hold it, and while it is on the keys show where each effect comes from. To chain with a mouse, Shift+click pattern, Shift+click each key but the last, then click the last. To copy, Shift+click write and pattern, then click the key. The same with the computer keys: Shift+8, Shift+p. esc lets every latch go. ?, or the ? in the display's corner, shows every key: space plays, , . tempo, < > swing, - = volume, t g y h the sliders, m this sheet.

On a key: the ochre frame is the step playing; the hatched band, the effect heard; a lit face, a step of the chosen sound; the small square in the middle, that key's sound sounding (green while it goes through the effect); the corner square, another sound's step; the bar, the chosen sound. Hold sound: lit faces are the sounds in use, and a dash marks a sample.
